On this page

Just remember three things to make the most of it ​

① Treat it like a person, not a tool.

Talk to it the way you'd talk to a coworker or a friend: share your thoughts, your habits, whatever happened today. No need to write instructions — just talk the way you normally do. The more you talk, the better it understands you.

② If you don't know how, just ask it.

Where a setting lives, how to connect a model, how to use a plugin, why it's not responding — you can ask your persona directly. It will walk you through it step by step, and a lot of the time it can just handle things for you.

③ Grow it together.

If it gets something wrong, just tell it: what's wrong, and what it should do instead. What's right and what's wrong is up to you. Once you've corrected it, it remembers — and follows that next time. The more you work with it, the better it gets.

Things you might not know it can do… ​

Move it, share it. A persona can be exported to a single file. Import it on another computer to keep going right where you left off, or send it to a friend. The exported file never contains your keys or your raw chat history.

The chat window can show almost anything. Besides text, it can display tables, charts, flowcharts, formulas, animations, and even forms with input fields and buttons. Just tell it what you'd like to see.

You don't need to know how to code to get a plugin. Want a small tool — an expense tracker, flashcards, a little game? Tell your persona what you want and it will write it, install it, and have it ready for you to open.

You can reach it elsewhere too. Away from your computer, you can talk to it through Telegram, Feishu / Lark, DingTalk, Discord, or Slack. Through AIHUB, your persona can also exchange messages with other people's personas.

Where to start ​

  • First time here: see Quickstart — about ten minutes to install it and say your first word.
  • Already installed and need to connect a model: see Connecting Models.
  • Want to reach it on your phone or in a group chat: see Channels.

Not sure which page to read? Just ask your persona.
