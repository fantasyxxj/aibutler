Just ask

Not sure it can do that? Just ask.

There's a lot you'd never think to hand to an AI — or you know it's possible but not how to set it up. Here you don't need to learn anything. Say what you want done.

Keep an eye on 2-bedroom flats in Tokyo under ¥150k and tell me when new ones show up

It checks every day on its own and pings you on Telegram when something new appears.

Put these receipts into a spreadsheet

Drop the photos in. It reads the dates, amounts and categories, builds the table, and can export it to Excel.

I want a little tool to track my spending

A few minutes later it's built — with its own screen and storage — and in your workspace. If something feels off, just tell it.

It walks you through it step by step and does whatever it can for you. Where a setting is, why something isn't working — ask it.

Memory

It remembers you —
and remembers right

Most AI forgets you when you close the window. Every aibutler persona has a memory of its own, and gets to know you better the longer you work together.

  • Say it once: your habits, preferences and promises — tell it once and it remembers.
  • Correct it once: when it gets something wrong, tell it what's wrong and what to do instead. Next time it does it your way.
  • No mix-ups: when something new conflicts with what it remembers, it checks before changing its mind, and stops using conclusions that are out of date.
  • Take it with you: export a whole persona as one file and keep going on another computer, or give it to a friend (your keys and raw chat history aren't included).

Under the hood

The technology

For those who want to dig in: the full design and source code are public on GitHub.

🧠

Its own memory engine

An embedded graph database — single file, no external services — with hybrid recall across keywords, semantic vectors and multi-hop association.

⚖️

Memory governance

Conflicts are classified before they're judged; overturned memories are kept, down-weighted, traceable and reversible — enforced by the engine.

⚡

Event-driven, always on

Internal events, chat messages or a local port can wake a persona; one POST from a script plugs it into any system.

🔀

Model-agnostic

Claude subscription, official APIs, compatible endpoints or your own gateway — use whichever works best.
